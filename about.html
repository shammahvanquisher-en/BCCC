Our Story

Beautiful Courts Christian Church was founded in 2001 under the leadership of its Founder and Senior Pastor, Pst. J.B. Tajeu.

The church began in Sita, moved to Mawanga, and eventually established its headquarters at Mawanga, Kwa Buda, Nakuru, Kenya. Through God's grace, the church has continued to grow and reach more communities through its branches and ministries.
