9TH–15TH NOVEMBER 2026

DOMINION SUMMIT 2026

PRAYERS WITH FASTING

FIRST WEEK

The first week of our 21 Days of Ascend Prayer and Fasting is dedicated to praying for DOMINION SUMMIT 2026.

01. SANCTIFICATION OF THE ALTAR

Father, in the name of Jesus, sanctify the entirety of BCCC main church altar, making it fit for your manifest presence at DOMINION SUMMIT 2026.

Psalm 24:1 " The earth [is] the LORD'S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein."

02. PROPHETIC SEASON OF GROWING AND PREVAILING

Father, in the name of Jesus, usher every participant into the prophetic season of "GROWING AND PREVAILING " through the insights of your word.

Isaiah 55:10-11 " For as the rain cometh down, and the snow from heaven, and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth, and maketh it bring forth and bud, that it may give seed to the sower, and bread to the eater: So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper [in the thing] whereto I sent it."

03. GREATER IMPACT OF DOMINION SUMMIT

Father, in the name of Jesus, let the impact of Dominion Summit 2026 be far beyond what we have ever known since inception, upon all the participants...both onsite and online.

Psalm 77:14 " Thou [art] the God that doest wonders: thou hast declared thy strength among the people."

04. THE CLOUD OF GOD'S GLORY

Father, in the name of Jesus, let your cloud of glory fill the entire Dominion Summit gathering thus producing permanent impact upon believers .

2 Chronicles 5:13-14 " It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers [were] as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the LORD; and when they lifted up [their] voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of musick, and praised the LORD, [saying], For [he is] good; for his mercy [endureth] for ever: that [then] the house was filled with a cloud, [even] the house of the LORD; So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud: for the glory of the LORD had filled the house of God."

05. PEACEFUL SUMMIT AND MANIFESTATION OF SIGNS

Father, in the name of Jesus, let Dominion Summit be the most peaceful event, thereby providing a platform for the manifestation of signs and wonders among your people.

Psalm 46:10 "Be still, and know that I [am] God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth."

06. PERFECT WEATHER CONDITIONS

Father, in the name of Jesus, we decree perfect weather conditions all through the summit; the sun shall not be scorching, and the cloud shall withhold its rain.

Job 37:22 "Fair weather cometh out of the north: with God [is] terrible majesty."

07. SANCTIFICATION OF ALL EQUIPMENT

Father, by the blood of Jesus, we sanctify all equipments that shall be used during Dominion Summit 2026 both the onsite and online platforms for excellent and hitch-free performance .

Hebrews 9:22" And almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission."

08. ANOINTING UPON PRAISE AND WORSHIP

Father, in the name of Jesus, let the praise sessions and choir ministrations be freshly anointed for maximum impact at Dominion Summit 2026.

Exodus 15:11 "Who [is] like unto thee, O LORD, among the gods? who [is] like thee, glorious in holiness, fearful [in] praises, doing wonders?"

09. SAFETY FOR ALL TRAVELLING PARTICIPANTS

Father, by the blood of Jesus, we decree safety for all those travelling from across the length and breadth of Kenya before, during and after Dominion Summit 2026.

Job 34:29 " When he giveth quietness, who then can make trouble? and when he hideth [his] face, who then can behold him? whether [it be done] against a nation, or against a man only:"

10. OPEN THE EYES OF EVERY PARTICIPANT

Father, in the name of Jesus, open the eyes of every participant of Dominion Summit 2026.

Psalm 119:18 " Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law."

11. EMPOWERMENT OF GOD'S MINISTERS

Father, by your Spirit, empower your servant and other ministers for maximum impact throughout Dominion Summit 2026.

2 Cor. 3:6 " Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life."

12. OPEN HEAVENS FOR UNUSUAL REVELATION

Father, in the name of Jesus, let the heaven over all those who will be ministering at Dominion Summit 2026 open up for unusual revelation, thereby imparting grace on all participants .

Act 19:20 .."So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed."

13. BREAKING EVERY BARRIER

Father, in the name of Jesus Christ,let every barrier on the path of this commission be crushed as you launch her into a new season of GROWING AND PREVAILING.

Isaiah 45:2 " I will go before thee, and make the crooked places straight: I will break in pieces the gates of brass, and cut in sunder the bars of iron:"

14. GATHERING BELIEVERS FROM EVERY CORNER

Father, in the name of Jesus Christ we release believers from all walks and From every corner of this country for the DOMINION SUMMIT 2026...we break every hinderance in Jesus name.

1 Thessalonians 2:18 " Wherefore we would have come unto you, even I Paul, once and again; but Satan hindered us."
